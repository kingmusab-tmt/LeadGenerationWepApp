# ShieldTech Solutions: Risk Assessment Report

## **1. Scope of Assessment**

### Focus Areas:

- Data Security
- Employee Cyber Awareness
- Network Infrastructure

### Timeframe:

Two weeks

---

## **2. Identified Risks**

### **External Threats**

1. **Phishing Attacks:**
   - Likelihood: High
   - Impact: Severe (compromise of employee credentials leading to data breaches).
2. **Ransomware Attacks:**
   - Likelihood: Medium
   - Impact: Severe (encryption of customer data leading to business disruption).
3. **DDoS Attacks:**
   - Likelihood: Low
   - Impact: Moderate (disruption of hosted services affecting client operations).

### **Internal Threats**

1. **Insider Threats:**
   - Likelihood: Medium
   - Impact: High (unauthorized access to sensitive data by employees).
2. **Data Misconfiguration:**
   - Likelihood: Medium
   - Impact: High (unintended exposure of client projects in cloud environments).

### **Physical Threats**

1. **Device Theft:**
   - Likelihood: Medium
   - Impact: High (loss of company laptops containing sensitive information).
2. **Unauthorized Physical Access:**
   - Likelihood: Low
   - Impact: Moderate (tampering with network devices in the IT room).

## **4. Mitigation Strategies**

### **For External Threats:**

1. **Phishing Attacks:**

   - Conduct company-wide phishing awareness training for employees.
   - Implement robust email filtering solutions to detect malicious emails.

2. **Ransomware Attacks:**

   - Maintain regular offline backups of critical data.
   - Deploy endpoint protection tools with anti-ransomware features.

3. **DDoS Attacks:**
   - Use a cloud-based DDoS protection service to filter malicious traffic.

### **For Internal Threats:**

1. **Insider Threats:**

   - Enforce role-based access controls (RBAC) to limit data access.
   - Implement employee activity monitoring tools.

2. **Data Misconfiguration:**
   - Regularly audit cloud storage configurations.
   - Use automated tools to detect and alert on misconfigurations.

### **For Physical Threats:**

1. **Device Theft:**

   - Use full-disk encryption on all company laptops.
   - Mandate the use of cable locks for devices in the office.

2. **Unauthorized Physical Access:**
   - Install access control systems for secure areas.
   - Use surveillance cameras to monitor physical spaces.

## **6. Recommendations**

1. Conduct quarterly cybersecurity awareness training for all employees, focusing on phishing and insider threats.
2. Invest in advanced endpoint protection and cloud configuration management tools.
3. Upgrade physical security measures, including access control and surveillance, for critical areas such as the IT room.

---

## **7. Conclusion**

ShieldTech Solutions faces several risks due to its reliance on digital systems and physical infrastructure. By implementing the above mitigation strategies and continuously monitoring the environment, the company can reduce vulnerabilities and maintain secure operations.
